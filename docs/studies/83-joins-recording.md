# #83: a 10-minute recording for joins at the top row

This recording gives the #83 study more top-row pairs: words that end on the top row, followed by a word that starts there, like "elephant ontario". About 10 minutes in TitanKeys Debug.

## Before you start (1 minute)

1. Open TitanKeys Debug, then **Settings > Typing log**.
2. Turn on **Record my typing**. Don't delete the log: it already holds the past weeks, and that's useful too.
3. Open a plain notes app. The log skips TitanKeys' own screens, and password, email, web address and private fields.

## How to swipe

- **Go at your normal pace.** Don't slow down or take extra care: the accidents are what we want to see.
- **Fix every mistake the way you usually do.** That's how the log tells a right join from a wrong one. If two words come out as one, delete it and swipe again.
- A new line between items is fine.

## 1. Top-row pairs (4 minutes)

Swipe each pair three times: first word, lift, second word.

- elephant ontario
- you were
- type quite
- write it
- were out
- out there
- your turn
- the price
- go to
- try it
- what if
- more power
- three people
- quite tired
- write your

## 2. Long words on the top row (2 minutes)

Each one three times, in one swipe if you can. If it breaks in two, fix it as usual.

- typewriter
- property
- territory
- pottery
- requirement
- opportunity

## 3. Sentences (2 minutes)

Each one twice.

- We were out there to try it.
- Write it quite quickly to the top.
- You were right to type your report.
- Pour it out to the property owner.
- Try to write your poetry out of town.

## 4. Long words across the middle rows, fast (1 minute)

Each one three times, as fast as you can.

- dashboard
- household
- flashlight
- landslide
- flashback

## When you're done

1. Back in **Settings > Typing log**, turn off **Record my typing**.
2. Tap **Export log** and send the file the usual way.

The log holds everything typed while it was on. Only numbers from it go into the study, never words.
