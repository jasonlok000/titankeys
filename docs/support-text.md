# Support TitanKeys: the words

Every line for the support flow. Prices come from Google Play, in local currency. Never pushy, never "best". Lines stay
around 60 characters, headings 4 words, tiles and buttons 1 or 2.

- **PRE:** anyone sees it, before paying. Coffee only: no orange, no juice.
- **SUPPORTER:** only someone who has paid.

The juice first shows in the thank-you. After that, only the supporter's own board says it.

- PRE **Title:** Buy me a coffee
- PRE **Line:** You made it to the bottom. Fancy a coffee?
