# 1.2.0

## App
- Emoji: Push Sym to type emoji from the strip.
- Better suggestions: The strip shows more words, correct spellings, and words in more fields.
- Delete a word: Push Alt, then push Backspace.

## New
- Emoji: Push Sym to show emoji on the strip. Touch an emoji to type it. To see more emoji, move your finger across the keyboard. Your recent emoji show first.
- Spelling: When you type with the keys, the strip can show the correct spelling of a word.
- Word suggestions: The strip shows more words for the letters that you type. Atlas finds these words in the full dictionary.
- Delete a word: Push Alt, then push Backspace.
- Short swipes for symbols: When Alt is on, a short swipe shows more symbols.
- Vibration: When you touch a word on the strip, the phone vibrates.
- Best word: The best word is in the middle of the strip.
- Suggestions in more fields: The strip shows words in address bars and private fields. Atlas does not learn words from these fields.
- Starter words: When you select all the text in a search bar, the strip shows starter words. Private search bars also show starter words.
- Next words: After you touch a word on the strip, the next words show immediately.

## Fixed
- Unwanted touches: When you type, a finger on the keys does not start a swipe. It also does not type a tap key.
- Suggestion bar: When you push Backspace or touch a key, the words stay on the bar until the new words show.
- Empty suggestion bar: In password, email, and number fields, Atlas does not show the suggestion bar.
- Space cursor: A finger that touches the space key immediately after a key press does not move the cursor.
- Screen readers: A screen reader reads the best word first.
- Tap keys: If you do not select tap keys, Settings shows Off.
