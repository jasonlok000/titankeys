# 2.0.0

## Swipe engine 2.0
- Trained for the Titan touchpad: Engine 2.0 adds three trained parts to the FUTO engine. A swipe reader, fine-tuned on real swipes from the Titan touchpad, finds more words. A small language model reads your sentence. A ranker, trained on Titan swipes, puts the best three words on the strip.
- More right words: On recorded sentences, the right word comes first 91% of the time, against 84% in 1.2. It is in the top 3 96% of the time, against 88%.
- Next words: Before you type, the strip shows the words that your sentence most likely needs next. After you type a letter or two, it shows the words that start with them and fit the sentence.
- On your phone: All models run on the phone. They load a few seconds after the keyboard starts, and swiping works in the meantime. If a model cannot load, the old engine works as before.

## Learn the keys
- Interactive guide: Seven chapters teach each feature on the real keys. Each one shows its gesture as soon as it opens.
- Quick setup: The guide opens each Settings page for you. When the switch is on, the guide comes back by itself.
- Playground: The app opens on a place to type and swipe freely, with a line of text to follow.
- Readable help: The guide page and these notes open one entry at a time.

## Typing
- Autocorrect for typed words: When you push Space, TitanKeys fixes the typo and also looks at the sentence. Backspace undoes a fix.
- Learns your next words: Word pairs that you type often come first on the strip. Private fields teach it nothing.
- Sentence starts: When the strip would be empty, it shows common first words.
- Hold a letter for its symbol: Hold a letter key to type its symbol. You feel a different vibration when the letter changes. It needs no other app.
- Modifier bar: When Shift, Alt or Ctrl is on, the bar above the keys shows it. A held key is filled, and a key pushed one time is an outline.
- Shift follows Alt: One push of Shift or Alt is for the next letter, and a held key stays on while you hold it.
- Fn as Ctrl: Fn works as Ctrl for shortcuts.
- Sentence capitals: The first letter of a sentence is a capital.
- TitanKeys is a word: You can swipe and type TitanKeys.

## Touchpad
- Steer and select from Space: Touch Space two times, keep the second touch down, and swipe up to move the cursor. Touch Space three times to select words. You feel a stronger vibration at each space.
- Delete more: Keep your finger down after a flick from Backspace to delete more words. A longer flick deletes faster.
- Resting fingers: A finger that rests on the keys does not type.
- Live gestures: Special gestures show in the strip while you make them.
- Word vibration: A picked suggestion and an autocorrect have their own, longer vibration.
- Apps outside text fields: The touchpad does not scroll apps when no text field is active. A setting turns scrolling on again.
- Back: When the touchpad is held, the first push of Back lets go of the touchpad and the second goes back.

## Symbols and quick settings
- Symbol and emoji pages: Sym shows symbols and emoji on pages that mirror the keys, in groups. Swipe up or down for more, or rest a finger on the bottom row to pick a group.
- Quick settings: In the Sym panel, push Alt to change settings with the letter keys. One of them changes the keyboard.

## Look and settings
- New look: A new icon, and an app in the orange of the Titan 2 Elite. Turn off orange accents in Settings for a keyboard in one colour.
- New settings: Swipe typing on or off, suggestions in an empty home search, and a typing log for tuning. The log is off at first and stays on your phone.
- New defaults: Hold a letter for its symbol, fn as Ctrl, suggestions in an empty home search, and American spelling are on at first.

## Fixed
- Back: The keyboard does not come back by itself after Back.
- Ctrl shortcuts: Ctrl+A and other shortcuts work on the first push.
- Shift: After you hold Shift for a word, the next word is not a capital.
- Address bars: The suggestion bar shows in address bars.
- Rounded corners: The bar and the Sym panel stay clear of the rounded corners of the screen.
- Cursor at the edge: The cursor keeps moving when your finger runs off the end of the touchpad.
- Word drag: When you lean the other way, the selected words go back one at a time.
