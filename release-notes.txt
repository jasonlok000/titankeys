# 2.0.0

## New
- Swipe engine 2.0: A second swipe reader, made for the Titan touchpad, finds more words. A small language model reads your sentence, and a ranker puts the best three words on the strip. If the new parts cannot load, the old engine works as before.
- Next words: Before you type, the strip shows the words that your sentence most likely needs next. After you type a letter or two, it shows the words that start with them and fit the sentence.
- Smarter typo fixes: When you push Space, the fix also looks at the sentence.
- Interactive guide: Seven slides teach each feature on the real keys. Each slide shows its gesture by itself if you wait.
- Quick setup: The guide opens each Settings page for you. When the switch is on, the guide comes back by itself.
- Playground: The app opens on a place to type and swipe freely, with a line of text to follow.
- Steer and select from Space: Touch Space two times, keep the second touch down, and swipe up to move the cursor. Touch Space three times to select words. You feel a stronger vibration at each space.
- Delete more: Keep your finger down after a flick from Backspace to delete more words. A longer flick deletes faster.
- Symbol pages: Sym shows all symbols in groups. Swipe up or down for more, or rest a finger on the bottom row to pick a group.
- Quick settings: In the Sym panel, push Alt to change settings with the letter keys.
- Modifier bar: When Shift, Alt or Ctrl is on, the bar above the keys shows it. A held key is filled, and a key pushed one time is an outline.
- Shift follows Alt: One push of Shift or Alt is for the next letter, and a held key stays on while you hold it.
- Back: When the touchpad is held, the first push of Back lets go of the touchpad and the second goes back.
- Orange accents: Turn them off in Settings for a keyboard in one colour.
- TitanKeys is a word: You can swipe and type TitanKeys.
- New defaults: Hold a letter for its symbol, fn as Ctrl, suggestions in an empty home search, and American spelling are on at first.
- Readable help: The guide page and these notes open one entry at a time.

## Fixed
- Rounded corners: The bar and the Sym panel stay clear of the rounded corners of the screen.
- Address bars: The suggestion bar shows in address bars.
- Word drag: When you lean the other way, the selected words go back one at a time.
- Cursor at the edge: The cursor keeps moving when your finger runs off the end of the touchpad.
