# 1.1.0

## New
- Cursor mode: Hold the space bar, then move your finger across the keyboard to move the cursor.
- Update notes: Open Atlas after an update to see what changed.
- Setup: Two new steps help you set the scroll assistant and turn on Atlas in Accessibility.
- Guide: The guide uses simpler words. A new part tells you what to do if Atlas does not operate correctly.
- Front page: The Guide and Settings buttons are now at the top of the page.

## Fixed
- Swipe back: One back swipe closes the suggestion bar and goes back in all apps.
- Capital letters: Atlas starts a field with a capital letter when the field has hidden characters at the start.
