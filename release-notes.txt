# 1.2.0

## New
- Emoji: Push Sym to show emoji on the strip. Touch an emoji to type it. Move your finger across the keyboard to see more. Your recent emoji show first.
- Spelling fixes: When you type with the keys, the strip can show the correct spelling of a word.
- More word suggestions: Typed words get suggestions from the full dictionary.
- Delete a word: Push Alt, then push Backspace.
- Shorter swipes for symbols: When Alt is on, a short swipe shows more symbols.
- Vibration: The phone vibrates when you touch a word on the strip.
- Best word in the middle: The best word suggestion is now in the middle of the strip.
- Guide: New parts tell you how to type emoji, use word suggestions and keep a copy of your words.

## Fixed
- Accidental touches: A finger that touches the keys while you type does not type a swipe or a tap key.
- Suggestion bar: The bar does not flash when you push Backspace.
- Screen readers: Screen readers read the best word first.
- Tap keys: Settings shows Off when no tap keys are selected.
