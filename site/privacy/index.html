TitanKeys

Privacy policy

For TitanKeys 2.1 and later. Last changed on 10 November 2026.

TitanKeys is a keyboard for the Unihertz Titan 2 Elite. It is made by Jason Lok, who is called "we" here. This policy tells you what TitanKeys keeps, what it downloads, what it sends, and how you control it. TitanKeys is open source, so you can check each part of this in its code.

In short

  • What you type stays on your phone. TitanKeys never sends it.
  • TitanKeys uses the internet for two things only. Both are strictly limited.
  • News: once a day at most, it downloads a short signed list of news from this website. It sends nothing about you. You can turn it off.
  • Swipe contributions: only if you agree, it sends examples of your swipes to train the swipe models. They are encrypted on your phone. You can stop and delete them at any time.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers, in the app or on this site.

What stays on your phone

These things are kept only on your phone, and TitanKeys never sends them:

  • The text that you type, and the text around it.
  • The words that TitanKeys learns from you, the word pairs that it learns, and your settings.
  • The clipboard, snippets and shortcuts.
  • The typing log, if you turn it on. It stays on your phone until you export it yourself.

The swipe engine and its language models run on the phone. TitanKeys learns nothing from password, email, web address or private fields.

Names on this phone

TitanKeys knows the names of the apps on your home screen as words, so you can swipe and type them with the right capitals. If you turn on Names from my contacts in Settings > Your words, and allow Contacts when Android asks, TitanKeys also reads the first and last names of your contacts, and nothing else about them. These names are only used on your phone to recognise the words you type. They are kept in memory, never saved, never learned, never included in contributions, and never sent anywhere. Turning the setting off, or taking away the Contacts permission, removes them at once.

If you remove TitanKeys, Android deletes all of this. Settings also lets you delete your words and the typing log.

What TitanKeys downloads: news

TitanKeys can tell you about a new version, a move to Google Play, or other important news. To do this, it downloads one file, feed.json, from this website at most once a day, and only when the phone has a connection.

  • What is sent: only an ordinary request for the file. It has no identifier, no cookie and no information about your phone. The app gives its name as "TitanKeys" and nothing more.
  • Who sees the request: this website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub sees the internet address (IP address) of the request. The GitHub privacy statement applies to that. We do not get your internet address.
  • How it is protected: the file is signed with our private key. TitanKeys ignores a file whose signature is not correct, so no other person can send you news through it.
  • How it shows: as a silent notification, only when you are not typing, with Snooze and Don't show again. The same news is on the News page in Settings.
  • How to stop it: turn off Show news in Settings > News. Then TitanKeys downloads nothing. You can also refuse the notification permission. Then the news shows only on the News page.

What TitanKeys sends, only if you agree: swipe contributions

You can help make swipe typing better for every Titan 2 Elite by sending examples of your swipes. This is off until you agree. TitanKeys asks one time, at the end of the guide, on a screen that tells exactly what is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Help train swipe typing.

What is sent

Only labelled swipe examples. Each example has:

  • The path of the swipe on the keys, with the time of each point from the start of the swipe, and the touch size for each point.
  • The word that you kept after the swipe, only if it is a word in the TitanKeys dictionary.
  • The 18 numbers that the swipe engine used to rank the words for that swipe. They are numbers only, without the words.
  • How you confirmed the word: you kept it, you chose it from the bar, or you corrected it.
  • The TitanKeys version, the version of the swipe models, the phone model (such as "Titan 2 Elite"), and whether the app came from GitHub or Google Play.
  • A random contributor ID. It is made on your phone when you agree. It is not connected to your name, your account or your phone, and it is only used to delete your examples when you ask. Settings > Help train swipe typing shows it, with a button to copy it.

What is never sent

  • The text around the word, and the text that you type with the keys.
  • Names and other words that are not in the dictionary. A swipe for such a word is not sent at all.
  • Anything from password, email, web address or private fields.
  • The clipboard, snippets and shortcuts.
  • Your contacts or their names, the names of your apps, your location, the apps that you use, or any identifier of your phone or account.

Why

Only to train and test the TitanKeys swipe models, so that they read swipes from more hands correctly. We do not use the examples for anything else. We do not sell them, show them to other people or use them for ads. The trained models are part of TitanKeys, which is open source. A model is made from many examples together and does not hold your examples.

How it is protected

  • Examples wait in a queue on your phone, up to 4 MB, about 1000 swipes. You can see how many wait and delete them.
  • TitanKeys sends them on Wi-Fi only, in batches. Before a batch leaves the phone, it is encrypted with our public key (X25519 and AES-256-GCM). Only our private key can open it. That key is kept offline, not on any server.
  • Batches go over HTTPS to a small server that we run on Cloudflare, which stores them in its own database. The server can store batches and delete them, and only we can download them, still encrypted, with a secret key. It cannot read them, and it keeps only the encrypted files. To stop abuse, it counts the requests from each internet address (IP address) for one minute at most. It uses the address only for that moment, in a hashed form, and never logs or stores it. Cloudflare carries the connection, as the Cloudflare privacy policy says.
  • The server files each batch under a hash of your contributor ID, so even the stored files do not show the ID.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until it is sent, or until you delete it. When the queue is full, the oldest examples go first.
  • On the server and with us: until they are used for training, and at most 2 years after they arrive. Then they are deleted. The server deletes its files after 2 years automatically.
  • Examples that we opened for training are kept only on our own offline computer, within the same 2 years.
  • At any time, on request, we delete all the examples for a contributor ID.

How to stop and delete

  • Stop: turn off Send swipe examples. TitanKeys stops collecting at once, and deletes the examples that still wait on your phone.
  • Delete waiting examples: deletes the queue on your phone.
  • Delete my contributions: deletes the queue on your phone, and asks the server to delete all the batches sent with your contributor ID. If your phone has no connection, it asks again when it has one. After this, new examples get a new contributor ID.
  • After you remove TitanKeys: removing the app deletes the queue and the contributor ID from your phone, and we never know who you are. So keep a copy of your contributor ID from Settings. To have your examples deleted later, send us the ID as the Contact section says, and we delete everything for it.

Children

TitanKeys is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is on this page with a new date, and the app tells you in its news. TitanKeys never starts to send a new kind of data without asking you first. Every change is in the history of this page.

Contact

For a question about privacy, or to ask about your data, email vignette@everyones-niche.com, or open an issue at github.com/jasonlok000/titankeys/issues.

To ask for deletion, send your contributor ID. An issue is public, so send the ID by email if you do not want it to show. While TitanKeys is on your phone, Delete my contributions in the app does the same at once.
