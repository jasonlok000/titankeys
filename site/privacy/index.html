TitanKeys

Privacy policy

For TitanKeys 3.0 and later. Last changed on 21 November 2026.

TitanKeys is a keyboard for the Unihertz Titan 2 Elite. It's made by Jason Lok, who is called "we" here. This policy tells you what TitanKeys keeps, what it downloads, what it sends, and how you control it. TitanKeys is open source, so you can check each part of this in its code.

In short

  • What you type stays on your phone. TitanKeys never sends it.
  • News: once a day at most, it downloads a short signed list of news from this website. It sends nothing about you. You can turn it off.
  • Bug reports: only when you press Send in the report form, it sends what you wrote, sealed so only we can read it.
  • Sharing: only if you agree, it sends examples of your swipes and daily totals of how well its features worked, to make TitanKeys better. Never what you type. It's encrypted on your phone. You can stop and delete it at any time.
  • Support: buying a coffee is optional and unlocks nothing. Google Play handles the payment. Only if you join the supporters board does TitanKeys send anything about it, and the board never shows an amount.
  • We don't sell or rent data.

What stays on your phone

These things are kept only on your phone, and TitanKeys never sends them:

  • The text you type, and the text around it.
  • The words that TitanKeys learns from you, the word pairs that it learns, and your settings.
  • The clipboard, snippets and shortcuts.
  • A bug recording, if you make one in Settings > Record a bug. It holds your touches, key presses and what the keyboard did, for 3 minutes at most, never in password or private fields. It doesn't save your text, but the keys and word choices it records can show most of what you typed while it ran. Only share it with people you'd show that to. It stays on your phone unless you send it to us with the report form, below, or share it yourself.
  • A crash report, if TitanKeys crashes or freezes: where in its code it happened, and the versions of TitanKeys, Android and your phone. Never anything you typed. The next time you open TitanKeys, it asks once whether to send it to us with the report form, and deletes it from the phone once it's sent or you say no.
  • A backup file, if you make one in Settings > Your words > Back up everything. It holds your settings, your words, the word pairs, snippets and shorthand, but not the clipboard or recordings. It isn't encrypted, and it goes only where you save it.

The swipe engine and its language models run on the phone. TitanKeys learns nothing from password, email, web address or private fields.

Names on this phone

TitanKeys knows the names of the apps on your home screen as words, so you can swipe and type them with the right capitals. If you turn on Names from my contacts in Settings > Your words, and allow Contacts when Android asks, TitanKeys also reads the first and last names of your contacts, and nothing else about them. These names are only used on your phone to recognise the words you type. They're kept in memory, never saved, never learned, never included in contributions, and never sent anywhere. Turning the setting off, or taking away the Contacts permission, removes them at once.

If you remove TitanKeys, Android deletes all of this. Settings also lets you delete your words and a bug recording.

What TitanKeys downloads: news

TitanKeys can tell you about a new version, a move to Google Play, or other important news. To do this, it downloads one file, feed.json, from this website at most once a day, and only when the phone has a connection.

  • What is sent: only an ordinary request for the file. It has no identifier, no cookie and no information about your phone. The app gives its name as "TitanKeys" and nothing more.
  • Who sees the request: this website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub sees the internet address (IP address) of the request. The GitHub privacy statement applies to that. We don't get your internet address.
  • How it's protected: the file is signed with our private key. TitanKeys ignores a file whose signature isn't correct, so no other person can send you news through it.
  • How it shows: as a silent notification, only when you aren't typing, with Snooze and Don't show again. The same news is on the News page in Settings.
  • How to stop it: turn off Show news in Settings > News. Then TitanKeys downloads nothing. You can also refuse the notification permission. Then the news shows only on the News page.

What TitanKeys sends, only if you agree: sharing

You can help make TitanKeys better for every Titan 2 Elite by sharing examples of your swipes and daily totals of how its features worked. One switch covers both: you share everything below, or nothing. This is off until you agree. The guide offers it at its end until you answer, and a short screen says what is shared before you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Sharing.

What is sent: swipe examples

Labelled swipe examples. Each example has:

  • The path of the swipe on the keys, with the time of each point from the start of the swipe, and the touch size for each point.
  • The word that you kept after the swipe, only if it's a word in the TitanKeys dictionary.
  • The 18 numbers that the swipe engine used to rank the words for that swipe. They're numbers only, without the words.
  • How you confirmed the word: you kept it, you chose it from the bar, or you corrected it.
  • The TitanKeys version, the version of the swipe models, the phone model (such as "Titan 2 Elite"), and whether the app came from GitHub or Google Play.
  • A random contributor ID. It's made on your phone when you agree. It's not connected to your name, your account or your phone, and it's only used to delete your examples when you ask. Settings > Sharing shows it, with a button to copy it.

What is sent: daily totals

While sharing is on, TitanKeys keeps daily totals on your phone of how its features worked, and sends one small encrypted batch a day. Only totals for the day, never what you did one by one, and nothing is counted in password, email, web address or private fields. A day has:

  • How many times TitanKeys' features acted, and how many of those you kept, swapped, undid or typed again: for example joined swipes, autocorrect, apostrophe and capital fixes, swipes by word length, words picked from the bar, Chinese and Japanese choices, and gestures.
  • How fast the keys showed for a field and how fast swipes were read, counted in rough bands.
  • Which settings you changed, by name only, never what you chose. Never your snippets, shorthand or word lists.
  • How far you got in the guide, and the slides you skipped.
  • How many times TitanKeys crashed or froze, without any details.
  • The TitanKeys, Android and model versions, the phone model, whether it came from GitHub or Google Play, the date (no time), and a random ID that changes every 30 days. That ID is never your contributor ID, and it isn't sent with your swipes.

Settings > Sharing > What's shared lists every total, with what it means, and shows today's so far. We may add totals of these same kinds for the same purpose. They show there first. We'll ask you again before we send any other kind of data or use it for anything else.

What is never sent

  • The text around the word, and the text you type with the keys.
  • Names and other words that aren't in the dictionary. A swipe for such a word isn't sent at all.
  • Anything from password, email, web address or private fields.
  • The clipboard, snippets and shortcuts.
  • Your contacts or their names, the names of your apps, your location, the apps that you use, or any identifier of your phone or account.
  • What time of day you type.
  • Anything the Accessibility service hears.

Why, and on what basis

Only to make TitanKeys work better: to train and test the swipe models, so that they read swipes from more hands correctly, and to tune autocorrect, joins, suggestions, gestures and the guide, and find crashes and slow spots. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can take back at any time in Settings. We don't use the examples or the totals for anything else. We don't sell them, show them to other people or use them for ads. The trained models are part of TitanKeys, which is open source. A model is made from many examples together and doesn't hold your examples.

How it's protected

  • Examples wait in a queue on your phone, up to 4 MB, about 1000 swipes. You can see how many wait and delete them.
  • TitanKeys sends them on Wi-Fi only, in batches. Before a batch leaves the phone, it's encrypted with our public key (X25519 and AES-256-GCM). Only our private key can open it. That key is kept offline, not on any server.
  • Batches go over HTTPS to a small server that we run on Cloudflare, which stores them in its own database. The server can store batches and delete them, and only we can download them, still encrypted, with a secret key. It can't read them, and it keeps only the encrypted files. To stop abuse, it counts the requests from each internet address (IP address) for one minute at most. It uses the address only for that moment, in a hashed form, and never logs or stores it. Cloudflare carries the connection, as the Cloudflare privacy policy says.
  • The server files each batch under a hash of your contributor ID, so even the stored files don't show the ID.
  • Daily totals are encrypted the same way, sent over HTTPS once a day on any connection, and filed under a hash of their 30-day ID. TitanKeys Debug, the test version, never sends them.

How long it's kept

  • On your phone: until it's sent, or until you delete it. When the queue is full, the oldest examples go first.
  • On the server and with us: until they're used for training, and at most 2 years after they arrive. Then they are deleted. The server deletes its files after 2 years automatically.
  • Examples that we opened for training are kept only on our own offline computer, within the same 2 years.
  • At any time, on request, we delete all the examples for a contributor ID.
  • Daily totals: on your phone until they're sent, 7 days at most; on the server and with us, 1 year, then they are deleted. The server deletes them after 1 year automatically.

How to stop and delete

  • Stop: turn off sharing. TitanKeys stops collecting at once, and deletes the examples and the daily totals that still wait on your phone.
  • Delete waiting swipes: deletes the queue on your phone.
  • Delete what I’ve sent: deletes the queue on your phone, and asks the server to delete all the batches sent with your contributor ID, and the daily totals of every 30-day ID your phone used in the last year. If your phone has no connection, it asks again when it has one. After this, new examples get a new contributor ID.
  • After you remove TitanKeys: removing the app deletes the queue and the contributor ID from your phone, and we never know who you are. So keep a copy of your contributor ID from Settings. To have your examples deleted later, send us the ID as the Contact section says, and we delete everything for it. The 30-day IDs of the daily totals go with the app, so we can't tell which totals were yours or find them; they're deleted after a year anyway.

What TitanKeys sends when you report a bug: the report form

Settings > Send feedback, Record a bug and the crash offer open a form in TitanKeys. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

What is sent

  • The kind of problem you picked, and what you wrote: what happened, and what you expected.
  • Your email address, only if you typed one, so that we can reply.
  • The TitanKeys version and build, your phone's model, the Android version, and when it was sent. The form shows these before you send.
  • A bug recording, only if one is attached in the form. You can take it off before you send. It holds your touches, key presses and what the keyboard did, as above, so it can show what you typed while it ran.
  • A crash report, only if the form came from the crash offer: where in TitanKeys' code it crashed or froze. Never anything you typed.

How it's protected and how long it's kept

  • On your phone, the report is put in one file and sealed with our public key, the same way as swipe contributions. Only our private key, kept offline, can open it. It goes over HTTPS to the same small server on Cloudflare, which stores it sealed and can't read it. The server counts requests the same way, to stop abuse.
  • We use it only to fix and improve TitanKeys, and to reply if you gave an email address. We don't share it.
  • On the server: until we download it, and at most 2 years. We delete reports we've dealt with.

When it can't be sent

With no connection, or if our server is busy, the report stays on your phone, and the form offers to email it instead. That opens your own mail app with the report attached and our address, titankeys@everyones-niche.com, filled in. Nothing goes until you send the mail yourself.

Support and the supporters board

TitanKeys is free. The Support page lets you buy the project a coffee, once or every month. It's optional, and it unlocks nothing.

  • Paying. Google Play handles every purchase and subscription. TitanKeys never sees your card or payment details. With each purchase, Google Play gets a random account ID: a hash of a random key that TitanKeys makes on your phone. It isn't your name, your email or your Google account.
  • On your phone. TitanKeys keeps a record of your purchases on your phone, to show your support total. The card that shows after ten days of typing counts your words, swipes, corrections, capitals and gestures on your phone. Those counts stay on your phone, and they're separate from Sharing.
  • Reading the board. While the Support page is open, TitanKeys downloads the board from our server. The request has no identifier.
  • Joining the board, only if you choose. Buying never needs the board. If you choose to join, TitanKeys sends our server the random account key, the Google Play purchase proofs, and the name and message you typed, if any. To show "This puts you at #N", it sends the account key and an amount. The server checks each purchase with Google Play, then keeps, for each account key: the product, the amount credited in cents, and hashes of the purchase token and the order ID. It never keeps the raw purchase token or order ID, your email, or your payment details.
  • What the board shows. Each supporter's place, and the name and message they chose to share. It never shows an amount. If you choose to stay anonymous, your support still counts, but you aren't on the public board.
  • Reports. Anyone can report a board entry. The report sends the entry's public ID and, if you write one, a reason. We read reports ourselves; nothing is hidden automatically.
  • How long it's kept, and how to delete it. Your entry and its purchase history stay until you tap Remove me on the Support page, or until we remove it. Remove me deletes both from our server. If you've lost the app, email us and we remove the entry.
  • Refunds go through Google Play. When Google tells our server about a refund, it lowers that supporter's total.
  • Against abuse. Our server counts "This puts you at #N" requests and reports from each internet address, by a hashed form of the address mixed with a secret, one counter for each hour. The counters are deleted within about a day. The address itself is never stored, and logging is off.

Your rights

If you're in the EU, the UK or another place with similar laws, you have the right to see, correct and delete your data and to get a copy of it, to object to how it's used, and to take back your consent at any time. Taking it back doesn't change what was done before. To use these rights, email us. Swipe examples are found by your contributor ID; daily totals only while TitanKeys is still on your phone, as above; a board entry by Remove me, or by email. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Our servers run on Cloudflare, which processes the data for us under its data processing terms. Sharing and reports are encrypted; the board's names and messages are public by your choice. It may do so in the US, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Children

TitanKeys isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is on this page with a new date, and the app tells you in its news. TitanKeys never starts to send a new kind of data without asking you first. Every change is in the history of this page.

Contact

For a question about privacy, or to ask about your data, email titankeys@everyones-niche.com, or open an issue at github.com/jasonlok000/titankeys/issues.

To ask for deletion, send your contributor ID. An issue is public, so send the ID by email if you don't want it to show. While TitanKeys is on your phone, Delete what I’ve sent in the app does the same at once.

To remove a board entry when you no longer have the app, email us and say which entry it is.
