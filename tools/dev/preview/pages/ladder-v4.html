Word ladder, v4

The word menu, finished as a mockup before anything is built. Its icons and motion are what ship: every move here is the move in the app, one to one.

Hold a word on the bar. I moves it one step up, O one step down, and Back closes. The menu stays open after every step. One-letter words open no menu, and symbols and emoji have no ladder.

Blocked⇄Not saved⇄Saved⇄Favourite
  • Favourite: always first. Solid, and now and then a faint heart rises from it.
  • Saved: TitanKeys knows the word. A dictionary word, a word it learned from your typing and a word you saved all count. Solid, with a faint book above it.
  • Not saved: TitanKeys doesn't know it. A light fill under a dotted outline: easy to read, clearly not kept.
  • Blocked: never suggested, learned or fixed into. The not-saved look in grey, struck through.

A resting finger shows the future. While a finger rests on I or O, the word shows in grey what that press will do. The press makes it real, in white or orange, with its effect.

Try it

Hold I or O to see the preview, and let go to press. Press again in the middle of an effect: it carries on from where it is. Back starts again from Saved.

Each step

Play runs the step at full speed, and Slow at a quarter. With Hover on, the finger rests first: you see the preview, then the press, then the result. The orange tick on the line under each step is its haptic frame, the one frame the phone buzzes on.

One motion language

All six effects share these. The springs are real physics (mass, stiffness and damping), so a second press picks up from the current position and speed instead of starting over.
