Word ladder, v3

Orchestrator's proposal for the word menu, refined after your review. Nothing here is on the phone yet. Hold a word on the bar: I moves it one step up, O one step down, and Back closes. The menu stays open after every step, and the hover stays on the key you pressed.

Blocked⇄Not saved⇄Saved⇄Favourite
  • Favourite: always first. The word is solid, with hearts.
  • Saved: TitanKeys knows the word. That covers a dictionary word, a word it learned from your typing (typed twice) and a word you saved. The word is solid.
  • Not saved: TitanKeys doesn't know it. The word is a dotted outline. new
  • Blocked: never suggested, learned back or fixed into. The word is a grey dotted outline, struck through.

One metaphor all the way through: the book, your dictionary. Save writes the word into it, and Unsave tears its page out. The icons are the ones that would ship: the app's own hearts and Back, plus a new book and block on the same grid and line weight. Particles use the same shapes, and the text is the app's Inter. One-letter words open no menu at all, and symbols and emoji have no ladder.
