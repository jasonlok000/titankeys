The bar at Normal size, 1:1, on your screen's real bottom edge. Words are swiped over and over (keyboard, quiz, titan, mob). Rows: how far apart the three key rows are drawn. Up: how far the middle row sits over the words' line. Edges: 1 draws the top and bottom of each row as far out as the keys reach; less pulls those edges in, so the rows can be farther apart without the trail running along the bottom edge.
